Agricultural systems and industries

Build a system map using authorised examples. Explain two connections between enterprises, industries, people, the economy, culture or society.

Evidence checklist
  • At least four system elements
  • Two explained interactions
  • Every current figure has a visible source and date

Data, marketing and value adding

Interpret one supplied trend, explain one responsible marketing choice and show how value adding changes a product or market opportunity.

Evidence checklist
  • Observation separated from explanation
  • Survey or marketing evidence considered
  • One justified value-adding decision

Task 1 — Topic Test preparation

Use Modules 1–5 to write a concise revision record: five key ideas, two cause-and-effect links, one data interpretation and one comparison you still need to practise.

Formal status: Task 1 is a 25% in-class Topic Test due Term 1, Week 10. The public site does not publish the test paper or answer key.

Plant structure, identification and performance

Label authorised plant structures, record the evidence used for one identification and explain how one characteristic may relate to pasture or crop performance.

Source boundary
  • Do not identify a plant from an ambiguous photograph alone
  • Separate observation from conclusion
  • Teacher directions control any sample collection

Soil, monitoring and agricultural technology

Choose two defensible soil or crop observations, explain what each can and cannot show, and justify how one technology supports a management decision.

Evidence checklist
  • Two observations with limitations
  • Technology linked to a measurement or decision
  • No invented calibration, rate or local procedure

Task 2 — Plant Collection + Research Task

Prepare the evidence pattern for five grasses, legumes/herbs and weeds, then select one from each category for researched descriptions and supported benefits and/or problems.

Formal status: Task 2 is worth 35% and is due Term 2, Week 9. The scope and sequence uses a conflicting Plant Density Trial title; the formal schedule/task set controls this pathway until the school confirms otherwise.

Prime lamb breeding, nutrition and feed evidence

Explain a source-supported breeding objective, compare two evidence inputs and connect nutrition or feed availability to a management decision.

Evidence boundary
  • No treatment, ration or reproductive procedure invented
  • Farm-specific values remain source- or teacher-controlled
  • Ethical and welfare implications are acknowledged

Prime lamb management, welfare, ethics and risk

Create a source-supported management sequence, then explain how technology, welfare evidence, ethics and risk thinking affect one decision.

Teacher-controlled boundary: current animal-handling methods, facilities, supervision, local WHS controls and observation criteria are not replaced by this page.

Task 3 — Prime Lamb report and handling skills

Prepare a breed-selection justification and portfolio evidence about consumer preference, farm type/distribution and market reports. Record teacher feedback for the separately observed practical component.

Formal status: Task 3 is worth 40% and is due Term 3, Week 9. The school schedule controls the outcome set; notification wording conflicts remain Teacher to confirm.

Dairy breeds, anatomy and the milk pathway

Use source evidence to explain one breed characteristic, one body-system relationship and the movement of milk through processing and marketing.

Evidence checklist
  • Breed claim supported by a named source
  • Anatomy labels used accurately
  • Processing steps do not invent operational settings

Dairy reproduction, technology and careers

Explain two connected reproductive or mammary-system ideas, evaluate one emerging technology claim and describe one source-supported dairy career connection.

Source boundary
  • No reproductive or animal-care procedure is prescribed
  • Technology claims distinguish capability from measured benefit
  • Career facts retain their source and date where current

Course synthesis and final evaluation

Select your strongest evidence from each term, explain one connection across production systems, evaluate one limitation and identify a specific next improvement.

Plans/drawings gate: no authoritative project plan or dimensioned drawing has been identified in the 2026 source set. Do not invent a farm, plant-trial, yards or dairy plan; any later source must be teacher-issued and provided with a visible Open larger link.
