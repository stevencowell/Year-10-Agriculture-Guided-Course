## Student-page implementation notes

- Group clips under the same four Drive topic headings, with a concise **Watch for** prompt and adjacent theory link for every card.
- Use `i.ytimg.com/vi/<id>/hqdefault.jpg` thumbnails, a keyboard-operable play control and click-to-load `https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/<id>?autoplay=1&rel=0`.
- Provide a visible **Open in YouTube** fallback for every clip, Close/Escape behaviour, focus restoration and a stacked 390 px layout without horizontal overflow.
- Revalidate all IDs immediately before any release; remove failures rather than leaving dead cards.

## Visible coverage gaps / blockers

The validated set is intentionally not padded. No sufficiently strong, short, current clip was retained yet for these source topics:

- Agricultural Systems & Management: Agriculture in Australia/interesting industries, livestock-data trend reading, marketing survey design, marketing strategies and alternative production systems;
- Pastures & Cropping: plant anatomy/identification, pasture deterioration, cropping finance, evaluation and careers;
- Prime Lamb Production: reproductive technologies, pasture sampling, genetic engineering/ethics and WHS/risk assessment; and
- Dairy Farming: cow/female/male reproductive anatomy, dairy deregulation and careers.

These are source-coverage gaps, not permission to add generic videos. The course theory and Busy Work can still cover them. If the parent requires video coverage for each, run a second targeted search after detailed source extraction provides the exact adjacent teaching emphasis.
