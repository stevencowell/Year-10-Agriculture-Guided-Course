# Year 10 Agriculture 2026 — outcome wording and map

## Applicable syllabus decision

For the 2026 course, the controlling syllabus is the official **Agricultural Technology 7–10 Syllabus (2019)**. NESA identifies 2025–2026 as planning/preparation for the newer **Agriculture Technology 7–10 Syllabus (2024)** and implementation from 2027; schools may choose early implementation. The Drive programmes, assessment schedule, notifications and tasks all use the 2019 `AG5-*` outcomes. Unless Wagga Wagga High School has explicitly authorised early implementation, retain the `AG5-*` framework for 2026.

### Boundaries on this map

- This is a **Year 10/2026-only** map. It does not establish full 100-hour or 200-hour course compliance because the Year 9 programme and the school’s hour model were deliberately not inspected.
- Programme-header outcome sets are recorded exactly. Individual lesson rows sometimes name additional outcomes not included in the header; they require a separate programme cleanup rather than silent expansion.
- AG5-10 is not declared in any of the four Year 10 programme headers despite explicit animal-welfare content in Prime Lamb and Dairy. **Teacher to confirm whether this is an omission or covered in Year 9.**
- Formal assessment coverage omits AG5-1, AG5-4, AG5-9, AG5-10 and AG5-11 in this Year 10 schedule. **Teacher to confirm whether these are assessed in Year 9, formatively evidenced, or require schedule adjustment.**
- Task 2 and Task 3 contain outcome-set conflicts; see `ASSESSMENT-METADATA.md`.

## Planning-only 2024 outcomes

Do not replace the 2026 codes without explicit early-implementation authority. The published 2024 Stage 5 outcomes are:

| Code | Exact wording |
|---|---|
| AGT5-SAF-01 | applies production processes and safe work practices in agricultural contexts |
| AGT5-APG-01 | explains factors that influence animal and plant selection, growth and development in agricultural enterprises and systems |
| AGT5-PRA-01 | explains industry practices in agricultural enterprises and systems |
| AGT5-ENV-01 | analyses relationships between agricultural production, sustainability and consumers |
| AGT5-IVT-01 | investigates and assesses the influence of innovation and management practices on agricultural enterprises and systems |
| AGT5-USE-01 | selects and justifies the use of technologies and processes in agricultural production and systems |
| AGT5-COM-01 | communicates ideas and concepts in agricultural technology contexts |

A one-to-one code substitution is not authorised: the 2019 fourteen-outcome structure and 2024 seven-outcome structure organise knowledge and evidence differently. Any migration requires a content-level crosswalk against the official 2024 focus areas and school implementation decision.
