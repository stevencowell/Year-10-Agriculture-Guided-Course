# Year 10 Agriculture guided course

A source-first Stage 5 Agricultural Technology website for the Wagga Wagga High School 2026 Year 10 programme.

The authorised `Year 10/2026` Google Drive subtree is the primary local authority. It supplies four term programmes—Agricultural Systems & Management, Pastures & Cropping, Prime Lamb Production and Dairy Farming—plus the school scope and sequence, assessment schedule, task packages and notifications. The course preserves those source boundaries and labels unresolved local facts `Teacher to confirm`.

## Student routes

- `index.html` — course landing page and 20 two-week modules
- `module.html?module=1` through `module.html?module=20` — three named sections, exactly ten checks per section, written evidence, autosave and Print / Save PDF
- `plans/index.html` — source-grounded prime-lamb pedigree-reading theory, full-resolution `Open larger` drawing and explicit broader-plan boundary
- `folio.html` — twelve-card autosaving and printable course folio
- `assessment/index.html` — confirmed formal task pathways and source-conflict status
- `busy-work/index.html` — bridge to the canonical shared Year 10 Agriculture Busy Work library
- `youtube-learning/index.html` — validated, purpose-linked Agriculture clips

Browser storage is specific to the current browser, profile and device. It prepares evidence; it does not submit work.
